RSSum for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Privacy Policy

The short version: RSSum does not require an RSSum account, include developer-operated advertising or analytics, sell personal information, or send your reading history to an RSSum-owned server. The app stores reader data locally and can use Apple iCloud sync. Feed websites, Reddit, AI providers and other services you choose receive the information needed for the feature you use.

1. Scope

This policy explains how RSSum handles information when you use the RSSum RSS and Reddit reader for iPhone, iPad or Mac. “RSSum,” “the app,” “we” and “us” refer to the app and its developer.

2. Information kept on your devices

RSSum keeps information locally so the reader works. Depending on the features you use, this can include:

  • Feed subscriptions, cached articles, article images, reading status, favorites and Reddit post status.
  • Reader preferences, refresh settings, display choices, voice settings and AI-provider settings.
  • API keys, Reddit OAuth tokens, usernames and local gateway settings that you enter or authorize.
  • Downloaded local models, generated audio and other feature caches.

RSSum does not operate a user-account system or an RSSum server that stores this information. Some locally configured secrets are protected with Apple Keychain; other settings are stored in the app’s local storage. Protect your device and any API credentials you enter.

3. Apple iCloud sync

When iCloud is available and the app’s sync features are used, RSSum uses Apple’s iCloud Key-Value Store to synchronize selected reader state across your Apple devices. This may include subscriptions, read and favorite states, Reddit post states, a locally generated device identifier and the device name used for sync coordination.

Apple controls iCloud storage and authentication. RSSum does not receive a separate server copy of your iCloud data. Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy apply to Apple’s handling of that information.

4. Feeds, articles and images

When you add or refresh a feed, RSSum requests the feed directly from the URL you provide. It may also request article pages, images, media, linked content and favicons from the hosts that publish them. Those hosts can receive ordinary network information such as your IP address, device or browser information, requested URL and request time.

RSSum may use Google’s favicon service to retrieve a site icon. The privacy practices of each website, CDN, image host or other service govern its handling of those requests.

5. Reddit

RSSum can read public Reddit content directly from Reddit. If you choose to sign in, Reddit’s OAuth flow can provide RSSum with an access token, refresh token and Reddit username so the app can perform the actions you authorize, such as loading authenticated content or voting.

Reddit receives requests, account and content information needed to provide those features. RSSum does not operate a Reddit proxy or sell Reddit data. Reddit’s privacy policy and terms govern Reddit’s handling of your information.

6. Optional summaries, AI and audio

AI and text-to-speech features are optional. The destination depends on the provider and mode you select:

  • On-device models: Processing stays on the device after a model has been downloaded. Downloading a model can contact its hosting provider, such as Hugging Face.
  • Google Gemini: RSSum can send the prompt and selected article or page text to Google’s Gemini API using the API key you provide.
  • OpenAI audio: RSSum can send text you choose to the OpenAI text-to-speech API when OpenAI audio is selected.
  • Web AI: RSSum can open ChatGPT or Gemini and pass the text you choose into that web service. The selected provider’s account and privacy settings apply.
  • Local Mac services: RSSum can send prompts or selected content to a summarization daemon or gateway at the host and port you configure, including a service running on your own Mac.

Review content before sending it to an external provider. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information unless you understand and accept that provider’s practices.

7. Analytics, advertising and tracking

RSSum does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising or cross-app tracking in this checkout, and the developer does not sell personal information. Websites and third-party services you visit or choose may use their own cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking technologies.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you remove it using the app or system controls, clear its caches or delete the app. iCloud-synchronized data is managed through your Apple account and iCloud controls. Tokens and credentials stored in Keychain may require separate deletion and should not be assumed to disappear merely because the app is uninstalled.

For information sent to a feed publisher, Reddit, Google, OpenAI, an AI web service, a model host or another third party, use that provider’s privacy controls and deletion process.

9. Security

RSSum uses Apple platform storage and authentication protections where available, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your devices updated, use a passcode, protect API keys and do not expose a local gateway to untrusted networks.

10. Children

RSSum is a general-purpose reader and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an RSSum-operated service.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as RSSum changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, email dealer.yen-61@icloud.com or open an issue in the RSSum GitHub repository. GitHub issues are public, so do not include passwords, API keys, OAuth tokens or private reading information.
